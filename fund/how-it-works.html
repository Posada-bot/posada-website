How it works

A public board where projects ask for support and backers send ADA straight to them. The platform never holds the money.
The idea

No committee. No custody.

Posada Fund is a board. A creator posts a project and publishes the wallet they want to be paid to. Backers send ADA directly to that wallet, on-chain, wallet to wallet.

Nobody approves your project, and no company holds the funds in between. The only thing the platform ever collects is a single flat fee to put a project on the board — it never takes a cut of what backers give, because the donations never pass through it.

If you’re raising funds

Listing a project

  1. Connect your wallet. Use any Cardano wallet through the standard browser connection. No account, no email, no password.
  2. Prove it’s yours. Sign a short message with your wallet. That signature is your login, tied to your stake key so you can always return.
  3. Write your project. Describe what you’re raising for, publish the wallet backers will pay to, and add a short video introducing the cause.
  4. Pay the one-time listing fee. A single flat fee, paid once in ADA, puts your project on the board. It’s confirmed on-chain before you can publish.
  5. Go live instantly. There’s no review queue. Your listing is public the moment the fee is confirmed.
  6. Receive support directly. Backers send ADA straight to your wallet. A live counter shows funding as it arrives on-chain.
  7. Report each month. Post a short monthly update on how funds are used. If reporting stops, your page goes dark until it resumes.
If you’re backing a project

Supporting a cause

  1. Find a project. Browse the board and open a listing. Watch the creator’s video and read what they’re raising for.
  2. Send ADA directly. Your support goes straight to the creator’s published wallet. It never touches Posada Fund.
  3. Verify it yourself. Because the wallet is on-chain, you can confirm in any explorer that your ADA arrived and how much a project has raised.
  4. Follow the updates. Monthly reports keep creators accountable after the money is sent, not just before.
Why it’s safe

The platform can’t touch your money

Donations only ever exist at wallets the platform doesn’t control. There is no shared account holding backer funds, so there is nothing for anyone — including the platform — to freeze, take, or lose.

This also means backers give at their own discretion. Posada Fund makes the money trail public and keeps creators accountable; it does not vet, endorse, or guarantee any project. Check the video, check the chain, and decide for yourself.

The fee

One flat fee, no cut of donations

Creators pay a single flat fee once, when they list. Whether a project raises a little or a lot, the fee is the same, and not a single lovelace of backer support is skimmed. The model is a board with an entry fee — not a payment rail that meters the flow.
