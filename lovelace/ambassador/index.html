Lovelace Ambassador Program

Earn $LILY. Grow the community. Build something real.

What is an Ambassador?

Lovelace Ambassadors are the people who bring real humans into the ecosystem. Every person you refer gets a better first experience — and you earn $LILY for every successful connection you make happen.

No pyramid schemes. No chain bonuses. One level deep, always.

How it Works

Get your referral link Unique to your account, found in Settings
Share it Your referred user signs up and activates their account
They get rewarded 35 $LILY added to their wallet on signup
You get rewarded 10 $LILY per qualified referral

Milestone Bonuses

Hit referral milestones in a single month and earn extra:

Monthly Referrals Bonus
10 referrals +10% on all referrals that month

Resets every month. Uncapped.

Extra Rewards

  • Founding Ambassador Badge — first 100 ambassadors earn a permanent badge on their profile
  • Leaderboard — top ambassadors featured monthly
  • Free Tier Upgrades — hit milestones, unlock membership tiers for free
  • Gift Multiplier — your Lily gifts carry extra weight in the matching algorithm
  • Early Access — new features before general release

Qualified Referral Rules

  • Referred user must sign up using your link
  • Must remain active for 30 days
  • One account per person — no self-referral
  • Direct referrals only — no chain or network bonuses

$LILY Payouts

All ambassador earnings are paid in $LILY at current DEX price. Tokens are credited to your in-app wallet as milestones mature. Withdraw anytime to your Cardano wallet.
