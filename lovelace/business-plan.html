  1. Vision & Problem

Web2 dating apps monetize user data, compromise privacy, and optimize for engagement over genuine human connection. They are fundamentally broken by design — their revenue model requires keeping users single and scrolling.

Lovelace is the flagship app of the Posada.io ecosystem: a privacy-enhanced, human-only dating platform built on Cardano, where data collection is minimized, identities are cryptographically verified on-device, and incentives reward meaningful behavior — not screen time. The architecture is designed for progressive decentralization, with user-owned data via Iagon integration planned post-mainnet launch.

The Posada.io ecosystem comprises five interconnected products:

  1. Lovelace — Privacy-enhanced dating app (primary product, São Paulo beta live)
  2. Byron — Decentralized social network (post-MVP)
  3. Shelley — Encrypted messaging layer (post-MVP)
  4. Posada Bot — Trading bot and analytics suite (discontinued 31 July 2026)
  5. $LILY Token — Utility token tying the ecosystem together on Cardano

Core technology stack:

  • Identus DID — Verified human identity with documents processed on-device and not transmitted to our servers (live on Android and iOS, end-to-end chain connected)
  • Halo2 ZK proofs — Privacy-preserving age verification, running server-side at beta. On-chain verification via Plutus V3 BLS12-381 planned for mainnet. Midnight integration post-Mohalu (Q2 2026+)
  • Iagon — User-owned, encrypted profile data storage (planned, not yet integrated)
  • Hydra — Instant micro-payments for gifts, tips, and rewards (post-MVP)
  • $LILY Token — Utility rewards token on Cardano. DEX listing at mainnet launch.
  • Aiken — CIP-0113 Programmable Token: 6 validators (issuance, transfer, admin, blacklist ×2, proof token). 24/24 tests passing. Audit pending.

Goal: Build Cardano's first mainstream-ready dating dApp — seamlessly onboarding non-crypto users through fiat-first PIX on-ramps (Transak, MoonPay) while delivering genuine privacy that Web2 competitors cannot match.

  1. Tokenomics — $LILY

The $LILY token is the economic layer of the Posada.io ecosystem. The gift Lily (🌸) = $1 USD worth of $LILY tokens. The token price floats freely on the DEX — the app dynamically calculates the $LILY amount per reward. Amounts always display as "$X in Lily" to abstract token mechanics from non-crypto users.
