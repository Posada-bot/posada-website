Delete Your Lovelace Account

We're sorry to see you go. You can delete your account and all associated data using either method below.

Option 1: In the App

Open Lovelace → Profile → Delete Account → Enter your password and type "DELETE" to confirm.

Your account and all data will be permanently removed immediately.

Option 2: By Email

Send an email to support@posada.io with the subject line "Delete My Account" from the email address associated with your Lovelace account.

We will process your request within 48 hours.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile, display name, and bio
  • All profile photos
  • Chat messages
  • Face verification data (embedding)
  • DID credentials (revoked)
  • $LILY balance and transaction history
  • Filter preferences and settings
  • Location data

What Gets Deleted Within 30 Days

  • Server backups containing your data

What Cannot Be Deleted

  • Your DID record on the Cardano blockchain is immutable by design. However, the associated credential is revoked, making it permanently unusable.
  • Anonymized account deletion logs (for fraud prevention — no personal data retained)

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you change your mind, you will need to create a new account.
