Treasury Governance

The Lovelace Treasury is structured for strong security, accountability, and clarity to the community and investors.

Multisig Structure

The Lovelace Treasury is managed via a 3-of-3 multisig wallet on Cardano.

Signers

  • Founder — overall project responsibility.
  • CEO/CTO — technical oversight & protocol alignment.

Rationale

  • Ensures no single person can move funds unilaterally.
  • Balances decision-making across business, technical, and financial domains.

Risk Management

  • At no point will more than 20% of Treasury assets be exposed in liquidity pools.
  • Remaining reserves held in secure, cold-storage multisig accounts.
  • Regular audits and quarterly reports published to the community.

Transparency

  • All Treasury transactions are on-chain and verifiable.
  • Multisig activity monitored and reported via a public dashboard.
  • Signer identities are transparent, reinforcing accountability.
