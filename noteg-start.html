Getting Started
NoteG is an autonomous Cardano trading bot you control from your phone. Setup takes about 10 minutes once you have ADA in a wallet. This guide walks you through everything from installing the app to placing your first trade.

Before you start

You'll need:

  • An Android phone (Google Play app — iOS not yet supported).
  • A real Cardano wallet with at least 150 ADA — Eternl, Lace, Daedalus, Yoroi, or Typhon.
  • An email address you can read from your phone.
  • About 10 minutes.
Why a real wallet — We strongly recommend NOT paying from Revolut, Wirex, Binance, Coinbase, or any similar fintech / exchange. Their ADA withdrawals batch every 30–90 minutes and your payment will arrive after the price-lock window expires. It will still work, but it makes onboarding slower and may need manual reconciliation. Real Cardano wallets confirm in under 90 seconds.
Step 1
Install NoteG from Google Play
  1. Open the Google Play Store on your phone.
  2. Search for "NoteG" and tap Install.
  3. Once installed, open the app.
  4. On first launch the app shows a Welcome screen. Tap "Subscribe" to begin. This opens your browser at the NoteG subscription page.
Step 2
Subscribe and pay

In the browser, you'll see a short form. Fill it out:

  • Your email address (the one you can read on this phone).
  • Your name.
  • Your country (we use this for VAT — required by law).
  • Payment currency: ADA or USDM. Pick whichever you have in your wallet.

Tap "Get quote". You'll see the exact amount to send, a Cardano receive address, and a countdown timer. The price is locked for 30 minutes.

  1. Open your Cardano wallet (Eternl, Lace, etc.).
  2. Tap Send. Paste the receive address.
  3. Enter the exact amount shown on the subscription page.
  4. Confirm and send. Cardano confirmations take 30–90 seconds.
Stay on the page — The status updates from "pending" to "paid" automatically once your transaction confirms. No need to refresh.
Step 3
Receive your tenant credentials by email

Within 2–3 minutes of your payment confirming, we send you an email with:

  • Your personal NoteG URL (looks like abc12345.posada.io).
  • An API token (a long hex string).
  • A wallet receive address — this is where you'll fund your bot.
Check spam — First-time emails sometimes land in spam. If you don't see it within 5 minutes, check the spam folder and mark it Not Spam.
Step 4
Pair your phone with your bot
  1. Open the URL from the email on this same phone OR on your laptop.
  2. An Auth dialog appears. Paste the API token from the email.
  3. Tap "Pair new device". A QR code appears on the screen.
  4. Open the NoteG app on your phone (the one you installed in Step 1).
  5. Tap "Pair" in the app, then scan the QR code.
  6. The app and the bot will show a 4-digit confirmation code. Make sure both show the same code, then tap Confirm on both sides.

Your phone is now paired. From now on, every change you make in the app (strategy parameters, dry-run toggle, manual sells) is cryptographically signed before it reaches the bot. Nobody can impersonate your phone.

Step 5
Fund your bot

Your bot has its own wallet on the server. Send ADA to the wallet address shown in the email (or in the app under Banking → Receive). You decide how much:

  • Minimum 150 ADA — below this, fees eat too much of each trade.
  • Sensible starter: 200–500 ADA.
  • Maximum: only as much as you'd be comfortable losing in a worst case.
Honest scope — The bot signs trades on the server. We take strong precautions, but anything held on the server is at higher risk than ADA in your personal wallet. Read the disclosure email we sent — and don't put in more than you can write off.
Step 6
Start trading

By default the bot starts in "DRY RUN" — it picks trades but does not actually send anything to the blockchain. You can watch it for a day or two to make sure you like its decisions.

  1. Open the NoteG app on your phone.
  2. Open the MODE card on the Home tab.
  3. Toggle DRY RUN → LIVE. Your phone will ask for biometric confirmation (fingerprint / face). Approve.

The bot is now live. Every trade will appear in the Activity Log in real time. From the app you can change strategies, adjust safety limits, pause individual positions, force a sell, or stop the bot entirely — anytime.

Refunds and support

If something is wrong with your purchase, email support@posada.io within 14 days. Include:

  • The transaction hash from your wallet (the long string after you sent).
  • The Cardano address you want the refund sent to.

We refund to the original sending address within 5 business days, minus the network fee.

What if I run into trouble?

Common issues and quick fixes:

  • Email never arrived: Check spam folder. If still not there after 10 minutes, email support@posada.io with your payment transaction hash.
  • QR code won't scan: Make sure the phone camera is focused. Move it slightly closer or further. Bright screen helps. Try refreshing the laptop browser to regenerate the QR.
  • Strategy toggle does nothing: Pull-to-refresh in the app. If the bot is still in DRY RUN you have to flip MODE to LIVE first before strategies can fire trades.
  • Bot says insufficient funds: Add more ADA to the bot's wallet, or lower the per-trade size in each strategy under Settings.
